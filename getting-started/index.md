# Getting Started with Contravis

Contravis is a contra dance choreography tool. You enter dance instructions (e.g. "neighbors balance and swing"), and it visualizes the dancers' movements.

## 1. Open the app

When you first open Contravis, you'll see a bird's-eye view of a bunch of dancers on the left, and an area on the right to write instructions for them.

![Empty dance](2b3141ce-2026-03-16.png)

## 2. Add your first instruction

Click the **+** button to open a text input. You can type dance instructions here.

![Text input appears for adding an instruction](92cf6346-2026-03-16.png)

## 3. Type an instruction

Type something like **neighbors balance and swing** and press Enter. Contravis tries to parse your text into an instruction. The visualization updates immediately -- you can see the dancers performing the move.

![Balance and swing instruction added](016c36a7-2026-03-16.png)

## 4. Add more instructions

Click the **+** button below your first instruction to add another. Type **long lines forward and back** and press Enter. As you type, Contravis offers autocomplete suggestions.

![Two instructions in the dance](bc31600a-2026-03-16.png)

You now have a 24-beat dance with two figures. The beat count and progression status are shown at the bottom of the instruction list.

## 5. Share your dance

Your dance is automatically saved in the URL. To share it, just copy the URL from your browser's address bar and send it to someone. When they open the link, they'll see exactly the same dance.

![Dance restored from a shared URL](e45e571b-2026-03-16.png)

No accounts, no saving (at time of writing) -- the entire dance is encoded in the URL.
