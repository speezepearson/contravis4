Title:     Pinwheel Petronella
Author:    Isaac Banner
Formation: Becket ccw

Figures:
   8 beats  balance &  petronella to long waves, gentlespoons face in ⁋
   8 beats  balance &  box circulate - gentlespoons cross while ladles loop right
   8 beats  balance &  box circulate - ladles cross while gentlespoons loop right
   8 beats  neighbors swing
   8 beats  balance &  petronella to short waves, partners take right hands, ladles left in the center
   4 beats  balance the wave
   4 beats  partners allemande right ¾
   8 beats  ladles start a half hey - lefts in center, rights on ends
   8 beats  partners swing
