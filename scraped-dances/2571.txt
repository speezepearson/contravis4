Title:     Dearfield
Author:    Isaac Banner
Formation: Becket

Figures:
   2 beats  slide left along set ⁋
   8 beats  star right - hands across - 4 places
   6 beats  mad robin, gentlespoons in front
   8 beats  gentlespoons start a half hey - lefts in center, rights on ends  - ladles ricochet
   8 beats  neighbors swing
   2 beats  gentlespoons allemande left ½ to trade
   4 beats  star left 2 places, ladles joining behind and ending across from neighbors
   2 beats  partners allemande left ¾
   4 beats  form an ocean wave & balance - gentlespoons by right hands and partners by left hands
   4 beats  trade the wave - dancers take one step forward, turn to face across, and neighbors pass by right shoulders across
  16 beats  partners balance & swing
