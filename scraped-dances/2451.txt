Title:     Winter in Winterland
Author:    Shannon Horst
Formation: Becket ccw

Figures:
   8 beats  long lines forward & back
   8 beats  ladles chain
   8 beats  circle right 4 places
   8 beats  zig right zag left (as gentlespoons start the zig out, roll away partners so gentlespoon is on the left coming into the next move) ⁋
   8 beats  next neighbors do si do once and a 1/4
   4 beats  form an ocean wave & balance - ladles by left hands and neighbors by right hands forward and back
   2 beats  pass through, walk forward to new neighbors ⁋
   2 beats  next neighbors pass by right shoulders (across the set, to set up a half hey)
   8 beats  gentlespoons start a half hey - lefts in center, rights on ends  - ladles ricochet (ladles ricochet when they meet to their partner on their home side)
   8 beats  partners swing
