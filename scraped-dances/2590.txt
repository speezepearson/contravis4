Title:     Dog Branch Reel
Author:    Bob Dalsemer
Formation: improper

Figures:
   8 beats  neighbors do si do once
   8 beats  ones swing
   8 beats  down the hall forward then backward and turn alone
   8 beats  up the hall and bend into a ring
   8 beats  circle left 4 places
   8 beats  neighbors swing
   8 beats  long lines forward & back
   8 beats  twos swing ⁋
