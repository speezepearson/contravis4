Title:     Everson Glen
Author:    Isaac Banner
Formation: Becket

Figures:
   8 beats  revolving door  -  ladles take right hands and drop off partners on other side
   8 beats  neighbors swing
   8 beats  right left through
   8 beats  mad robin, ladles in front (CCW)
   4 beats  ladles dance in to a long wave in the center
   4 beats  ladles turn alone, return to place
   8 beats  circle left 3 places single-file, ladles turn over right shoulder to face partners
  14 beats  partners meltdown swing
   2 beats  slide left along set ⁋
