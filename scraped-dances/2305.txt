Title:     Bobbing for Isaacs 
Author:    Isaac Banner
Formation: improper

Figures:
  16 beats  neighbors balance & swing
   8 beats  gentlespoons start a half hey - lefts in center, rights on ends  - gentlespoons ricochet to start, rather than crossing the set, ladles dance a standard half hey
   0 beats  form long waves - gentlespoons face in, ladles face out (right hands w/ partners)
   8 beats  balance &  box circulate - gentlespoons cross while ladles loop right
   8 beats  balance &  box circulate - ladles cross while gentlespoons loop right
   8 beats  partners swing
   8 beats  ladles chain to neighbors
   8 beats  ladles start a half hey - rights in center, lefts on ends to next neighbors ⁋
