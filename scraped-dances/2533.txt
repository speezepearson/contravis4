Title:     Clotted Cream
Author:    Aden Water
Formation: Becket

Figures:
   2 beats  slide left along set
   6 beats  circle left 3 places
   8 beats  neighbors swing
   8 beats  long lines forward & back
   4 beats  ladles pull by right to partner
   4 beats  partners allemande left once leisurely until ladles face in
  16 beats  ladles start a full hey - rights in center, lefts on ends
  16 beats  partners balance & swing ⁋
