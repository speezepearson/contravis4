Title:     Bicycle
Author:    Sam Whited
Formation: Becket

Figures:
   4 beats  balance the ring
   4 beats  circle left 2 places
   8 beats  Oval left
   8 beats  Oval right, end across from new neighbors ⁋
   8 beats  star left - hands across - 4 places
   8 beats  ladles start a half hey - rights in center, lefts on ends  - gentlespoons ricochet (ladles pull by and gentlespoons cast off to start)
   8 beats  neighbors swing
   4 beats  balance the ring
   4 beats  ladles give & take partners
   8 beats  partners swing
