Title:     Quesadilla on the Half Shell
Author:    Shannon Horst
Formation: improper

Figures:
  16 beats  neighbors balance & swing
   8 beats  gentlespoons allemande left 1½
   8 beats  partners swing, end facing down the hall
   6 beats  down the hall and bend into a ring (gentlespoon 2, ladle 2, gentlespoon 1 ladle 1)
   2 beats  pass through across the set, reform line across coming up the hall (ladle 1, gentlespoon 1, ladle 2, gentlespoon 2)
   8 beats  up the hall and bend into a ring
   8 beats  circle left 3 places
   2 beats  pass through ⁋
   6 beats  next neighbors do si do once
