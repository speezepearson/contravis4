Title:     Fulford Harbour
Author:    Isaac Banner
Formation: Becket ccw

Figures:
   8 beats  revolving door  -  ladles take right hands and drop off partners on other side
   8 beats  neighbors promenade along the set on the left (CW)
   2 beats  ladles cast back one place, gentlespoons walk forward to next neighbors ⁋
  14 beats  next neighbors meltdown swing
   8 beats  next neighbors promenade along the set on the right (CCW)
   8 beats  gentlespoons allemande left 1½
  16 beats  partners balance & swing
