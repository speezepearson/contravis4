Title:     Hedgehog’s Favorite
Author:    Isaac Banner
Formation: Becket

Figures:
   4 beats  balance the ring
   2 beats  pass through
   4 beats  gentlespoons gate partners to face into the set
   6 beats  circle right 3 places
   6 beats  zig right zag left to next neighbors, dropping hands with partners ⁋
  10 beats  neighbors swing
   8 beats  long lines forward & back
   2 beats  pass through across the set
   6 beats  circle left 3 places single-file, gentlespoons turn back to face partners
  16 beats  partners meltdown swing
