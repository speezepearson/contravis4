Title:     It Started on Facebook
Author:    Michael  Fuerst and Nicholas Rockstroh
Formation: Becket CCW

Figures:
   8 beats  right left through
   8 beats  left diagonal ladles chain
   8 beats  star left - wrist grip - 3 places
   8 beats  partners do si do once (end in long wavy lines, gentlespoons facing in, partners right hands joined,  gentlespoons facing a new neighbor's back.)
   8 beats  balance and box circulate (gentlespoons cross set, ladles loop right to where partner had been; gentlespoons now facing out, new neighbors right hands joined)
   4 beats  balance (right and left)
   4 beats  promenade 3 places CW aroumd group of 4 (actually takes 6-8 beats)
  12 beats  partners swing (12-14 beats to swing or balance and swing)
