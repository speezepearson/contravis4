Title:     Retrouvaille
Author:    Seth Tepfer
Formation: triad (triple minor dutribecket)

Figures:
   8 beats  hands six, into the middle and back
   8 beats  first corners swing
  16 beats  second corners balance & swing
   8 beats  partners right hand balance &  box the gnat
   8 beats  three changes of rights and lefts, starting partner right
   8 beats  three more changes of rights and lefts
   8 beats  partners swing ⁋
