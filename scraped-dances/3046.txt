Title:     Valerie Did Not Write This Dance
Author:    Sam Whited
Formation: Duple becket

Figures:
   8 beats  slice left and straight back
   8 beats  right left through, power courtesy turn to end facing down the hall
   8 beats  down the hall and turn as a couple
   8 beats  up the hall and bend into a ring
   8 beats  balance &  petronella
   8 beats  neighbors swing
   4 beats  balance the ring
   8 beats  Ladles pull partner to your home side into a swing ⁋
