Title:     All Hands On Deck (inverted)
Author:    Dugan Murphy
Formation: improper

Figures:
  16 beats  neighbors balance & swing, then pull by hey:
  16 beats  ladles start a hey - rights in center, lefts on ends - until gentlespoons meet the second time, gentlespoons allemande right once
  16 beats  partners balance & swing
  10 beats  gentlespoons do si do 1½
   6 beats  neighbors gyre left shoulders once ⁋
