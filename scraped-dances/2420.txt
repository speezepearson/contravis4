Title:     Cut to the Jack (for Annie)
Author:    Moose Flores
Formation: Becket

Figures:
   8 beats  slice left and straight back ⁋
   8 beats  gentlespoons allemande left 1¼ around while the ladles orbit clockwise ½ around
   4 beats  gentlespoons form a long wave in the center - balance the wave, then "cut the diamond" ...
   4 beats  partners pass by left shoulders
   8 beats  ladles allemande right 1½
  16 beats  neighbors balance & swing
   2 beats  circle left 1 place
  14 beats  partners swing
