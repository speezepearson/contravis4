Title:     Roll for Initiative
Author:    Alex Burka
Formation: duple becket (reverse double progression)

Figures:
   8 beats  right diagonal right left through (find new neighbors across, leaving the RL-thru neighbors behind; if you have nobody on your right diagonal stay in place) ⁋
   8 beats  ladles allemande right 1½
  16 beats  neighbors balance & swing
   8 beats  ladles chain
   4 beats  balance the ring
   4 beats  gentlespoons roll away partners with a half sashay (up or down the set, ladles in front)
   4 beats  balance the ring
   4 beats  ladles roll away neighbors with a half sashay (across the set, gentlespoons in front)
   8 beats  partners swing
