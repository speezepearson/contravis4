Title:     Lavender Weave
Author:    Conor, Danae, Lee, Arthur (at LCFD Fall Camp 2024)
Formation: Becket

Figures:
   8 beats  right left through
   8 beats  slice left, and on the way back, robins roll your partner away ⁋
   8 beats  ladles start a half hey - lefts in center, rights on ends  - gentlespoons ricochet when meeting in the middle
   8 beats  neighbors swing
   8 beats  long lines forward & back
   8 beats  mad robin, ladles in front, clockwise
   8 beats  gentlespoons start a half hey - lefts in center, rights on ends  - ladles ricochet when meeting in the middle
   8 beats  partners swing
