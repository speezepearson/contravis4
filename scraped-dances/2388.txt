Title:     Folklife Noodles
Author:    Isaac Banner
Formation: improper

Figures:
   2 beats  pass through to next neighbors ⁋
   6 beats  ladles start a half hey - lefts in center, rights on ends - ladles pull by left to start
   8 beats  neighbors swing
   8 beats  circle left 3 places
   8 beats  partners swing, end facing down
   8 beats  down the hall and turn alone
   8 beats  up the hall and bend into a ring
   8 beats  pass through to an ocean wave & balance - gentlespoons by left in the center, partners by right on the sides
   2 beats  partners allemande right ½
   2 beats  ladles allemande left ½
   4 beats  form an ocean wave & balance - ladles by left hands and neighbors by right hands
