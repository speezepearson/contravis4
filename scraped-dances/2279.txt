Title:     Companion Piece
Author:    Isaac Banner
Formation: Becket

Figures:
   8 beats  circle left 3 places (single-file, optionally)
   8 beats  neighbors swing
   4 beats  pass through across the set and turn alone
   4 beats  balance the ring
   8 beats  ladles chevron into neighbors place—ladles pass right shoulder to trade places, then fall back straight across the set while gentlespoons cast up or down into their neighbors’ place
   6 beats  half poussette - ladles pull partners back then left
  10 beats  ladles start a hey - lefts in center, rights on ends - until partners meet the second time (RL, PR, LL, NR, RL)
  14 beats  partners meltdown swing
   2 beats  slide left along set ⁋
