Title:     This Dance Came to Me in a Dream
Author:    Alex Burka
Formation: duple becket

Figures:
   8 beats  slice left and straight back ⁋
   8 beats  gentlespoons chain (can make it an open chain by substituting allemande R for courtesy turn)
   8 beats  star right 4 places
   8 beats  neighbors do si do once
  16 beats  neighbors balance & swing
   8 beats  gentlespoons start a half hey - lefts in center, rights on ends  - ladles ricochet
   8 beats  partners swing
