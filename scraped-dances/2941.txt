Title:     Thatcher Pass
Author:    Isaac Banner
Formation: proper triplet

Figures:
   8 beats  ones right hand balance &  box the gnat (couple 1)
   2 beats  ones pull by right across the set
   6 beats  couple 1 cast down one place, couple 2 move up
  16 beats  ones contra corners
   2 beats  ones pass by right shoulders along in the center
  14 beats  parallel heys for three - ladle 1 pass ladle 2, gentlespoon 1 pass gentlespoon 3 by right shoulders to start
   2 beats  ones pass by right shoulders across
   6 beats  couple 1 cast down one place, couple 3 move up
   8 beats  partners swing across, end facing up
