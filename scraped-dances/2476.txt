Title:     Stick With The Union (for Dorian and Chris)
Author:    Aden Water
Formation: Becket

Figures:
   8 beats  gentlespoons allemande left once. Immediately...
   8 beats  partners promenade across and a little wide to progress ⁋
   8 beats  ladles allemande right 1½
   8 beats  neighbors swing
   8 beats  mad robin, gentlespoons in front
   8 beats  half poussette - ladles pull partners back then left.
   8 beats  facing star clockwise 3 places with gentlespoons putting their left hands in and backing up
   8 beats  partners swing
