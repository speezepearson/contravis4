Title:     Red &amp; Green Chilly
Author:    Michael Barraclough
Formation: Improper

Figures:
   2 beats  neighbors pass by right shoulders
   6 beats  next neighbors gyre left shoulders once
   2 beats  neighbors pass by right shoulders
   6 beats  prev neighbors gyre left shoulders once
   4 beats  neighbors allemande right ¾
   2 beats  gentlespoons pass by right shoulders
  10 beats  partners swing
   4 beats  ladles give & take neighbors
  12 beats  neighbors swing
   8 beats  square through two - partners balance &  pull by right, then neighbors pull by left, face in
   8 beats  square through two - neighbors balance &  pull by right, then partners pull by left to progress to new neighbors
