Title:     Stir Crazy
Author:    Erik Weberg
Formation: Becket

Figures:
   6 beats  circle left 3 places
   2 beats  pass through
   8 beats  next neighbors swing
   8 beats  circle left 3 places
   8 beats  partners do si do once
  16 beats  square through four - partners balance &  pull by right, then neighbors pull by left, then repeat
  16 beats  partners balance & swing
