Title:     Helgesson Hall
Author:    Isaac Banner
Formation: improper

Figures:
   8 beats  gentlespoons allemande left 1½
   8 beats  partners swing
   8 beats  circle left 3 places
   8 beats  neighbors swing
   8 beats  ones half figure 8 below and step into a line of 4 across
   8 beats  down the hall and ones turn as a couple (twos turn alone)
   8 beats  up the hall and bend into a ring
   4 beats  balance the ring
   4 beats  partners California twirl ⁋
