Title:     Frederick Reel variation
Author:    Tom Hinds
Formation: Becket

Figures:
   8 beats  down the hall and turn as a couple
   8 beats  up the hall and bend into a ring
   8 beats  circle left 3 places
   8 beats  neighbors swing
   8 beats  long lines forward & back ⁋
   8 beats  ladles allemande left 1½
  16 beats  partners balance & swing
