Title:     Sail Back to Aptos
Author:    Alan Kline
Formation: Becket

Figures:
   8 beats  ladles chain
   8 beats  circle right 4 places
   2 beats  turn alone toward partners ⁋
   4 beats  circle left 2 places
  10 beats  neighbors swing
   4 beats  partners pass by right shoulders across and continue facing out
   2 beats  sail back - raise right arm overhead while backing up, turn to catch right hands with partners
   2 beats  ladles take partners
   8 beats  partners allemande right 1½, ladles face across
   8 beats  ladles start a half hey - lefts in center, rights on ends
   8 beats  partners swing
