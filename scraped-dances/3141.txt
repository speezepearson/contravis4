Title:     Reverse Thanksgiving
Author:    Breaking Up Thanksgiving Dance Writing Workshop
Formation: Becket ccw

Figures:
   8 beats  neighbors see saw once (across the set)
   8 beats  circle right 4 places
   8 beats  ladles start a half hey - rights in center, lefts on ends, give left hand to your partner
   8 beats  star left 3 places ⁋
   8 beats  next neighbors allemande right once, and form a ring
   8 beats  balance &  petronella
  16 beats  partners balance & swing
