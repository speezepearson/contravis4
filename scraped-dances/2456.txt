Title:     Jaded
Author:    Cary Ravitz
Formation: improper

Figures:
  16 beats  neighbors balance & swing
   4 beats  gentlespoons allemande left ½
   4 beats  star left 2 places
   8 beats  partners gyre left shoulders once
  16 beats  partners balance & swing
  16 beats  gentlespoons start a full hey - lefts in center, rights on ends  - ladles ricochet first time, ladles ricochet second time, second ricochet is rotational clockwise (as if passing right) into progressed position ⁋
